Switch between three stages of publishing an article. Use the arrow keys to move between tabs.

Write your title and section headings before adding screenshots or code.

Read the generated page and try its links and controls with the keyboard.

Open the public URL after deployment and verify that it serves the latest article.

Blockquotes

A blockquote sets a passage apart from the surrounding prose. Add a footer when the reader needs to know where the quotation came from.

Preview the article at the width your readers are likely to use.

Check the preview with a keyboard before signing off.

Alex Rivera, Sample editorial checklist

Footnotes

Keep short explanations in footnotes when they would interrupt the article. Follow a note number to read it, then use its backlink to return.

Preview the draft locally,1 check its public URL after deployment,2 and sign off after a keyboard pass.3

Lists

Use an ordered list when the sequence matters, and bullets when readers can work through the items independently. A definition list pairs terms with explanations.
