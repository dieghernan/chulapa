This page groups categories from the `demo` collection. Select a category to
jump to its group, then open one of the listed demos.

Actual selection for this demo:

```yaml
layout: cloudcategory2
permalink: /demo/category
include_collection: demo
```

For your site, use [Add tag and category indexes](../docs/63-tag-category-clouds).
Options and compatibility aliases are in the [layout reference](../docs/04-layouts#cloud-tags-and-categories).
The [complete page source](https://github.com/dieghernan/chulapa/blob/main/docs/_pages/demo_category.md) includes its header and breadcrumb settings.
This demo uses the `cloudcategory2` compatibility alias of `cloudcategory`.
