Browse the tagged guides and references below. Coverage is partial and includes appearance, content editing, publishing, site search, installation, navigation, site identity, branding, comments, analytics, custom hooks, feeds and accessibility. Installation includes migration, starter examples and theme updates; identity and branding include menu/footer links, site details, favicons, fonts, icons and publisher metadata. Comment providers and installation methods are alternatives, not sequential steps. Google Analytics and GoatCounter can run together; the built-in feeds and jekyll-feed can coexist at different addresses. Syntax and variable references join their corresponding content and appearance topics. The FAQ retains its question-based directory; both tutorials retain their step-by-step progression. For all documentation, use the documentation index.
